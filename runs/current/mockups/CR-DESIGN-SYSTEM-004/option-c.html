The count appears at both ends. That is the point of this option — after reading 200 rows you can see where you are and move on in the same glance. The cost is the same sentence twice on one screen, and the two are not quite identical: only the top one is read aloud, because announcing every page turn twice over would be worse than not announcing it at all. The rows-per-page chooser still appears only once, at the top.
