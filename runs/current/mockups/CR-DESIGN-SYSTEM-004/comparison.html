Paging controls for long lists — three options

Built once in the shared kit so every list works the same way. All three drawings below use the arrangement you asked for. They differ in one thing only: where the “Showing 1–25 of 312” line sits.

Settled, and the same in all three: the rows per page chooser sits at the top right of the list, and the back and forward arrows appear twice — top right and bottom right, so you can move on from either end of a long list. The chooser is never repeated at the bottom; one list, one setting.

True of all three, whichever you pick

  • The chooser offers 25, 50, 100 and 200 — and choosing one takes you back to the first page, so you can never land on a page that no longer exists.
  • The last page shows the remainder honestly. 312 rows, 25 at a time, ends on “Showing 301–312 of 312” — twelve rows, never padded out to look full. 100 rows at 25 a page is four pages, not five with an empty one.
  • An empty list still says so — “Showing 0 of 0”, with both arrows dimmed, rather than a blank space.
  • Back is dimmed on the first page and forward on the last, in both the top and the bottom row.
  • Everything is reachable by keyboard, and the count is read aloud after each page turn — from the top row only, so a page turn is never announced twice over.
  • The search and filter row above the list does not change at all, on any screen that has one today.
What happens after you pick. This builds the control in the shared kit only — no screen changes the day it lands. Each list gains it later, in its own separate piece of work, and each app must also move to the new version of the kit first. The transaction-lists job is waiting on both of those steps, in that order. Nothing is slow today — this is preparation, not a repair.
