Read aloud, and reachable by keyboard

Tabreaches “Rows per page”, then Previous, then Next at the top — then the list, then the two arrows at the bottom
Enter Space ↓opens the size list; ↑ ↓ move; Enter chooses
Enter on Nextmoves a page — and the line “Showing 26–50 of 312” is read out, so there is proof it worked
A dimmed arrowis skipped by Tab and announced as unavailable
The two sets of arrowsare announced as “list paging” and “list paging, end of list”, so someone listening knows which end they are at

said once, not twice The sentence is read aloud from the top row only. If both rows announced it, every page turn would be spoken twice over — so the count is announced in one place, even in the option that prints it in two.
