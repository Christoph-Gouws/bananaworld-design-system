Option C — everything starts ticked, and you untick what you don’t want

The familiar spreadsheet way. When nothing is being hidden, every room shows a tick and the top row reads “3 of 3”. You narrow the list by unticking the rooms you do not want, rather than by ticking the ones you do.

Untick any single room and the last row — the one with no room recorded — silently goes with it, because it is not one of the rooms you left ticked. It has no tick of its own to keep.

Why this is not the recommendation. Three ticks against a box that says “All rooms” looks like a filter is already on when it is not, and the row with nothing recorded has no entry in the list, so it disappears the moment you untick anything. It also needs a second new idea — a “(blank)” entry — before it is honest, which is a bigger change than the fault we are fixing.
