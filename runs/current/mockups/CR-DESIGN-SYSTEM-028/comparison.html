Portas

In every option: space 1 is on the left behind the cab and space 2 on the right. The last drop is the block at the cab and the first drop the block by the doors; the screen says which space belongs to which drop, the picture only draws the blocks. A truck with one drop shows no drop lines. A taken space never shows another packhouse's pallet number and cannot be tapped. "Picked" is the first tap of a move: tap a pallet, then a free space. Every space is at least a fingertip high (48 px). The drop lines and the warning past a standard truck are written in words, not shown by colour alone, so they survive a black-and-white print. The picture never makes the page slide sideways.
