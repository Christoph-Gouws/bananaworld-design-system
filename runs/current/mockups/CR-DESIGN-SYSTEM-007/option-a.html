Six fields line up along the top in one instruction. The buttons box, which its author put in the middle on purpose, stays in the middle. Nothing was silently thrown away.

2 · The order of who wins

1This one box, said the modern way — always wins
2This one box, said the older way — wins over anything said about the whole line
3The whole line — applies to every box that said nothing about itself
4Nothing said at all — sits in the middle, as it always has

The rule in one sentence: the more specific instruction wins. Rung 2 is the one being fixed — today the whole-line instruction beats it and no one is told.

Why this one

It is what was agreed when this option was first approved, it is what anyone building a screen expects, and it is the smallest of the three changes.

  • Nothing anyone sees today moves. No app is using the whole-line instruction yet.
  • Nothing is added or removed. No new setting appears; the apps take this up whenever they choose.
  • The older way of writing it keeps working — which matters, because it was the only way to say this until last week.
