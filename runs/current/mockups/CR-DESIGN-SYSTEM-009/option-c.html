Why this is not the recommendation. It is the answer you already turned down on the wrapping question, and making the writing smaller does not rescue it: one long note in one row widens that column for the whole report, and the figures you actually came for end up off the screen. Sliding sideways stays available as a safety net either way — it just should not be the everyday behaviour.
