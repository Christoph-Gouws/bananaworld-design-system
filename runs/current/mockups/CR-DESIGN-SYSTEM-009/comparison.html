A size is a ceiling, not a fixed width. A column never takes room it does not need: “Cold room 1” still sits in a narrow column even where it is allowed an ordinary one. That is why the report does not end up looking like a grid of equal boxes.

Nothing here needs a decision — but check one assumption

I am assuming names, descriptions and addresses get the roomy size, most columns the ordinary one, and dates, references and numbers the tight one. When you open a real report you can name any column you want more or less room for — that is a small adjustment, not a rebuild.
