The little "waiting" ring in menu rows: where should it sit?

When someone clicks a link and the next page takes a moment, a small ring turns beside what they clicked. In menu rows, rows with a count, and the big tablet buttons, the ring today nudges things out of place. All three options below stop that. They differ only in where the ring sits in those rows.

Same in A, B and C: nothing moves while you wait. Links inside ordinary sentences keep the ring just after the words, exactly as today. The thin yellow line at the top of the page shows as before. Quick page changes show nothing at all.
