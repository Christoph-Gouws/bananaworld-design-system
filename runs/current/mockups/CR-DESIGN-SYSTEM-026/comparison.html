The shared headline figure box — pick a look

One figure box for every app, starting with the four boxes at the top of the packhouse end-of-shift screen. Each option shows the same figures: a normal shift on a phone, a shift with nothing recorded yet, another app's colour, and a dark look. The box with the small arrow opens the waiting list when tapped; the others are only for reading.

In every option: the coloured line along the top is decoration only. A warning such as "2 with no temperature" is said in words, and the orange only backs it up. A screen reader reads the name and the figure together, then the line underneath. A dash means the screen says nothing has been recorded; a real zero prints 0.
