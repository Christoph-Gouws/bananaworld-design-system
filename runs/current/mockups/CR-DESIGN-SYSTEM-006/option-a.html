The row is told “line up on top”, and the single tick box in front is told “not me — stay in the middle”. Without that escape, a small square control stranded at the top of a tall row looks like a mistake.

Why this is the recommendation

It matches the shape of the problem. Being level is a property of the whole line, not of six separate boxes.

  • One instruction per line, not one per box. A wide grid has six or seven boxes and the same answer for all of them.
  • Nothing can be half-done. There is no “I updated five boxes and missed the sixth”, which is exactly how a row ends up crooked in the first place.
  • The exception still exists where it genuinely reads better — a tick box or a buttons column.
  • Off unless asked for. Every existing list in every system carries on exactly as it is today.
