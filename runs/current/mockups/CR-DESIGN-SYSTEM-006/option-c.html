The tick box in front and the buttons at the end are small, single controls with nothing under them. Pinned to the top of a tall line they read as knocked out of place rather than lined up. There is no way to say “everything except these two” — it is all or nothing.

3 · Which forces the old habit back

Faced with that, whoever builds the screen goes back to the workaround we have today: leave the grid alone and hand-build the awkward boxes outside the shared kit. That is exactly how the depot system's own order screen ended up written by hand — the thing this change is meant to stop.

Where this lands

The shortest instruction of the three, and the only one with no escape hatch.

  • One sentence for a whole grid — nothing to repeat, nothing to miss.
  • No exceptions at all, including for the small controls that plainly want to stay centred.
  • Pushes people back to hand-built screens the moment one box needs to differ.
