A thin yellow line while the next page is on its way

Each option shows the same moment on a desktop and a tablet (both shrunk to fit): someone clicks Users while on Organisations. The pictures repeat every six seconds. A still of the "waiting" moment sits underneath. Only the line's movement differs between A and B. C adds one thing.

Same in A, B and C: the current page stays put until the new one is ready, and nothing jumps. A small spinner turns just after the word you clicked. Quick page changes show nothing at all. The tablet gets a slightly thicker line and a bigger spinner. People who have asked their device for less movement see a still line (bottom of this page).

A: the line fills up Recommended

The line grows from the left, slowing as it goes, then shoots to the end and fades the moment the new page arrives. It reads like "almost there".
