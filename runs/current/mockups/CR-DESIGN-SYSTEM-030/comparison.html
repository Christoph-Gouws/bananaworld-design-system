Printing inside the slide-out: pick a layout

The same delivery note in every option. It opens inside the app's slide-out, and nothing opens in a new window or tab. The note here is a stand-in. The words and buttons are examples, and each app supplies its own English and Portuguese words.

Option A — Note first, buttons underneath Recommended

You read the note, then act. The two buttons sit side by side at the bottom, where a thumb reaches them on a phone.
