On a narrower window the strip wraps to two across. With three boxes the document number lands bottom left — the opposite corner from where it belongs. Fixing this properly means changing how the strip lays itself out, which this change was asked not to do.

Why I advise against this

  • It breaks a ruling you already made — the document number top right.
  • It cannot be fixed without re-laying-out the strip, and re-styling the strip is outside what this change was asked to touch.
  • “Four boxes, this order, every form” is what the standard means. Letting the count vary per app makes it a suggestion rather than a standard.
  • Nobody has asked for it. The stuck change needs a word, not a hole.
