Number pad and big buttons for the market tablet

Two new pieces for the shared screen kit, so the market station screens can be built. Try the number pads: they work on this page. The figures are made up.

The same in all three

  • Keys 1 to 9, then Clear, 0 and delete. Each key is finger-sized for gloves. Clear and delete are grey.
  • Whole crates only, at most four digits. Typing 0 then 5 reads 5.
  • The big buttons: a picture over a short name, an optional small number, and a dark style for the main actions. Long names wrap onto two lines; they are never cut off.
  • The two figure boxes use the kit's existing card. Nothing new is needed for them.
